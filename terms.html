Description of Service

TradieTick is an iOS app that helps Australian homeowners assess tradie quotes. You can paste or photograph a tradie's quote, and the App will provide an indicative analysis covering: a price-fairness assessment, identification of missing or unusual items, and suggested questions to ask the tradie. The App may also link out to official Australian government licence registers so you can verify a tradie's licence directly with the licensing authority.

Indicative Analysis Only — Not Professional Advice

The analysis provided by TradieTick is informational and indicative only. It is not financial advice, legal advice, trade advice, valuation, certification, or any other form of professional advice. Real prices for tradie work vary considerably based on access, materials, urgency, regional differences, individual tradie overheads, and many other factors that cannot be fully assessed from a written quote.

You should always obtain multiple quotes for major work and consult appropriately qualified professionals before making significant financial decisions. TradieTick is a second-opinion tool and does not replace your own judgement.
